⚡ 3-Step Instant Setup

1

Connect CYD via USB

Use a data-capable Micro-USB or USB-C cable plugged into Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera.

2

Click Install

Click the Install button next to any verified project firmware below.

3

Select COM Port

Pick your ESP32's serial port (CH340 or CP2102) from the browser dialog.

Missing USB drivers? 📥 CH340 Driver (Windows) 📥 CP2102 Driver (Mac/Win)
🔧 Flashing Troubleshooting & Tips
  • Serial Port Not Showing? Ensure your cable is a data cable, not a power-only charge cable. Install the CH340 or CP2102 driver above.
  • "Failed to connect / handshake timeout"? Hold down the onboard BOOT button on your CYD while clicking Install, then release once flashing starts.
  • Using Linux? Add your user to the serial group: sudo usermod -a -G dialout $USER and restart your session.
  • Prefer manual flashing? Click the ⬇️ .BIN button next to any firmware to download the compiled binary for esptool, VS Code, or ESP-IDF.
