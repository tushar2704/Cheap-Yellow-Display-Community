## 🛠️ General 3D Printing Guidelines

- **Filament**: PLA is great for desktop stands; PETG or ABS is strongly recommended for wall mounts near sunlight or in warm enclosures.
- **Tolerances**: All models are engineered with 0.25mm dimensional clearance for standard FDM 3D printers.
- **CAD Source**: Parametric STEP files are provided so you can modify cutouts for your specific sensors or battery packs in Fusion 360, FreeCAD, or Onshape.
