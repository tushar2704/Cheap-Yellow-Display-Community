## 🟢 Beginner Projects Gallery

### 1. [01 — Hello CYD Hardware Suite](../projects/beginner/01-hello-cyd/)
- **Display**: 320×240 Landscape
- **Visuals**: Dark navy background with signature CYD yellow header banner, live touch crosshair tracking, and real-time LDR ambient light gauge.
- **Hardware Verified**: TFT, Touch, RGB LED, LDR sensor, Speaker chime, MicroSD slot.
- **Enclosure Pairing**: [60° Desktop Wedge Stand](../enclosures/cyd-2.8-desktop-stand/)

### 2. [02 — Touchscreen WiFi Spectrum Analyzer](../projects/beginner/02-wifi-scanner/)
- **Visuals**: Channel scan list with color-coded RSSI signal bars (Green: > -60 dBm, Yellow: -60 to -75 dBm, Red: < -75 dBm), encryption badges, and a tactile "SCAN NOW" touch button.
- **Video Tutorial**: [Watch on IamNotIntoTech](https://youtube.com/@IamNotIntoTech)

### 3. [03 — Stylus Touch Paint](../projects/beginner/03-touch-paint/)
- **Visuals**: 8-color swatch toolbar with white selection border, continuous line drawing, canvas clear button, and synchronized RGB LED lighting.

### 4. [04 — RGB Touch Sliders & Mixer](../projects/beginner/04-rgb-sliders/)
- **Visuals**: Red, Green, and Blue touch slider tracks with circular thumbs, live blended 16-bit color preview swatch, HEX code readout, and hardware PWM control.

### 5. [05 — MicroSD Digital Photo Frame](../projects/beginner/05-sd-slideshow/)
- **Visuals**: Auto-advancing picture slideshow, photo counter badge, and tap-left/tap-right navigation.

### 6. [06 — Tactile Button Matrix](../projects/beginner/06-touch-buttons/)
- **Visuals**: Dual-column latching toggle buttons ("LIGHT", "FAN", "ALARM") with active state glow, audible chirps, and tap counter card.
